Ambient OS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

What this service is

Ambient OS is a personal note-taking and productivity assistant operated by and for a single individual. The operator sends notes and questions to the system by SMS and receives replies containing their own previously stored information.

Who uses it

There is exactly one user: the account owner, who is also the operator and developer. There is no public sign-up. Messages from any number other than the owner's are rejected.

Mobile information is never shared

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with any third party for any purpose other than delivering the service itself. Text message originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party.

Data collected

The system stores the content of messages the owner sends to it, along with message metadata (timestamp, message identifier). This data is stored in a private database controlled solely by the owner and is not transmitted to any third party except as required to deliver SMS messages (Twilio) and to generate replies (Anthropic).

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends entirely on how often the owner chooses to message the system. Typical volume is fewer than 50 messages per day. The system only replies to messages the owner initiates; it does not send unsolicited messages.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help.
