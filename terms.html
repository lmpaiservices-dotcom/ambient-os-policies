Ambient OS — Terms and Conditions

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Service description

Ambient OS is a personal note-taking and productivity assistant. The account owner sends notes and questions by SMS and receives replies containing their own previously stored information.

Eligibility

This service is operated by and for a single individual. It is not offered to the public and accepts messages only from the owner's registered mobile number. All other inbound numbers are rejected.

Consent

The owner consents to receive messages by configuring their own mobile number in the system's settings as the sole authorized sender and recipient.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on the owner's use. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
