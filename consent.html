Ambient OS — SMS Consent

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Ambient OS is a personal note-capture and recall service. To receive text messages from Ambient OS, submit your mobile number below and give consent. Numbers are added to the service allowlist by the operator after review. Ambient OS currently has one registered account holder.

Request SMS registration



This checkbox is optional and unchecked by default. You are not required to consent to text messages to submit this form, to contact Ambient OS, or to use any other part of this site. SMS consent is separate from acceptance of the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

Message frequency

User-initiated only. Ambient OS replies to messages the account holder sends to it and does not send unsolicited messages. Frequency varies.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. Reply START, YES, or UNSTOP to resume.
